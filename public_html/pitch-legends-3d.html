What I built.
Why it helps.

Built from the ground up with Three.js and a custom physics engine, Pitch Legends 3D bridges classic stadium 11v11 matches and enclosed 5-a-side night cages (Yaoundé Cage, Lagos Rooftop, Rio Cage and Tokyo Neon) where the ball rebounds off perimeter walls without stopping play.

On defense, touch buttons automatically adapt into Slide Tackle, Shoulder Barge, Press and Standing Tackle, while graphics presets (Cool 30 FPS Battery Saver, Balanced 45 FPS and High 60 FPS) prevent overheating on phones.

Cameroon Legends front and center

Headlined by Samuel Eto’o (#9, OVR 99) and Roger Milla (OVR 96) with a custom Cameroon #9 app icon and national squad.

5-a-side night cages

Switch seamlessly between full grass stadiums and continuous walled urban cages illuminated under floodlights.

Built for real phones

Adaptive thermal FPS caps, dynamic DPR scaling and spaced landscape touch controls keep sessions cool and responsive.
